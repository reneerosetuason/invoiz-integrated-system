INVOIZ · RETURN / REFUND GUIDE · Updated 9 November 2026

Buyer-fault returns: agree, return, refund directly.

The seller retains the agreed original seller-funded costs and deducts the new return delivery. Only the new delivery fee goes into platform escrow. The seller sends the net refund directly to the buyer.

FlowExampleProposalBuyerSeller depositTransportReceiving return payDirect refundSaved bank detailsAdmin payoutsOther cases

Three separate flows

Agreement: Buyer requests return → Seller proposes buyer responsibility → System calculates original cost recovery and new delivery → Buyer accepts, suggests changes or declines → Seller revises until both agree.

Parcel and refund: Buyer saves receiving account + Seller submits delivery deposit receipt → Collection authorized → Seller schedules pickup → Buyer-side rider → Buyer-side hub → That hub's inter-hub driver, if needed → Seller-side hub → Seller-side delivery rider → Seller confirms physical receipt → Seller sends net refund directly → Buyer confirms actual money received.

Delivery money: Seller deposits the agreed new delivery fee → Admin confirms escrow received in Manage Payments → Completed service share becomes ready → Admin uses Release payout → Recipient confirms in regular Remittances → Normal confirmed earnings update.

No admin proposal approval, responsibility decision or manual return-fee quotation is required. Collection authorization is separate from bank verification. A screenshot is not verified escrow money: payouts remain blocked until the actual deposit is confirmed. Rejected receipts pause further custody until corrected.

The confirmed example

AmountMeaning
₱4,599.00Eligible item value.
₱619.90Recorded original commission and delivery costs: admin ₱459.90, pickup ₱30, origin hub ₱30, inter-hub ₱20, destination hub ₱30, last-mile ₱50.
Less ₱50.00Credit for shipping already paid by the buyer. Do not charge it twice.
₱569.90Original seller-funded costs retained as seller compensation. This is cost recovery, not a new payout or a lost-profit penalty.
₱160.00New return delivery, at the saved Commission rates for the reverse route.
₱3,869.10Seller sends directly to buyer: ₱4,599 − ₱569.90 − ₱160.
Seller deposits ₱160Only the new delivery goes to platform escrow. Seller does not deposit the ₱3,869.10 refund or another ₱569.90 compensation payout.

The seller's original net was ₱4,029.10. In this example, ₱3,869.10 direct refund + ₱160 return deposit uses that amount. Real transfers are made in the user's banking/GCash app; Invoiz records receipts and confirmations, it does not initiate a bank transfer.

For partial returns, original commission is allocated by eligible item value, original transport and original shipping credit by returned quantity. Previous accepted returns cap remaining original costs and shipping credit so they cannot be recovered twice. If deductions consume the refund, the proposal is rejected and the parties must choose another remedy.

Reverse transport and automatic earnings

Finding assignments in the rider app: Rider app 1.0.8 (build 10) includes a Return jobs card on Home and Deliveries. It shows the assigned return number and opens the collection address and Confirm collection button. Inter-hub drivers have a Return transfers card on Home. Refresh the card or pull down to fetch a new assignment. The return job shows its collection/delivery address. The existing assignment remains linked to the selected worker.

Next step: The return center shows the current action as text with its own button. After pickup assignment, Change assigned rider is collapsed because the next action belongs to the rider. Handling hub and assigned rider names are shown. Reassign collection rider only when another rider must take over.

Destination logistics hub: NorthStar, when it handled the original seller pickup, is recommended for the return. Keep it selected or choose another available receiving hub. AILYN remains the collection hub, and its company supplies the online inter-hub driver. A destination change is recorded in the agreement history and moves only unstarted destination services; agreed money and the customer refund do not change.

Online before assignment: Logistics can select only online, approved riders from the correct hub for return pickup, delivery back to the seller and replacement delivery. Inter-hub return drivers must also be online. If none are online, ask the rider/driver to use Go Online in their app and refresh the page. Availability is checked again when submitting, including zero-priced services. A rider going offline after assignment can still record the existing handoff; this does not cancel assigned work or change its earnings.

At rates of ₱30 pickup, ₱30 origin hub, ₱20 inter-hub, ₱30 destination hub and ₱50 final delivery, a one-item cross-hub return costs ₱160. Actual amounts are snapshotted from Commission settings; pickup is multiplied by returned quantity. Changing settings later does not reprice accepted terms.

Issues, seller-fault remedies and existing paid cases

Buyer-fault issue: Report a payment or parcel issue sends a message to the other party and pauses further customer steps. The reporting buyer/seller can use Resolve my reported issue after resolving it together. Admin cannot replace their agreement. Before deposits, use Suggest changes or Decline instead. After a real receipt/collection, agreed deductions cannot be silently edited.

Seller fault: Existing flow is preserved: seller pays all configured return services separately, without deducting them from the buyer refund. Original refund funding must be available or restored through the existing seller refund-funding receipt and actual-bank verification. Payment staff records the customer refund under that existing flow. Refund-only/partial-refund keeps the item with buyer and creates no return delivery; exchange retains replacement stock, dispatch and buyer receipt confirmation.

Cancelled or unused delivery money: Only verified, genuinely unused money may be returned to the actual depositor. In-transit cases cannot be cancelled through a budget shortcut. A refund of unused money is not worker earnings.

Return #4 remains under its recorded paid agreement. Its buyer already deposited ₱160 and the seller restored ₱4,599 for the earlier platform refund. Those payments and accepted terms are not converted into new deductions or charged again. New proposals on new cases/untouched drafts use the policy above. Test the new flow on a fresh return; no paid case was reset.

Response deadlines are reminders, not automatic acceptance, forfeiture, collection or refund. No guide statement claims an actual bank transfer happened without its required evidence/confirmation.
