Returns, refunds and who gets paid

This guide uses the labels you see in the application. The amount and responsibility must be agreed before anyone collects a return. Money transfers are manual bank/e-wallet transfers; screenshots require verification.

Start here: where to click

Open Return center, find the case, and press View agreement, fees & funding. Its title is Return agreement & funding. Use Return payments & earnings for the separate return remittances. Admin also has a link under Manage Payments.

Do not mark an item physically returned when the buyer keeps it. Refund-only cases waive custody and never restore stock or create return transport earnings. A refund transfer being sent is separate from the buyer receiving it.

ResolutionWhat actually happens
Return and refundCollect the item, track hubs/transport, inspect at the seller, then send the funded cash refund.
Refund only — buyer keeps itemNo transport or stock restoration. Buyer agrees to keep the item; admin sends the agreed refund.
Partial refund — buyer keeps itemBuyer agrees to a lower refund for the defect or accepted condition. No physical return.
Exchange — return and replacementReturn the original, inspect it, record replacement dispatch and rider, then buyer confirms the replacement. No cash refund; replacement delivery has its own quoted service line.

Buyer: every field and button

What you seeWhat it means / what to do
Request returnChoose the exact purchased variation and quantity, explain the issue and attach evidence. “Ordered wrong size/color” means you selected incorrectly. “Received wrong size/color” means the seller sent a different variation.
Eligible item valueThe maximum item refund for this quantity, based on the original paid price after applicable discounts.
Agreed item refund / Original shipping reimbursementThe proposed cash components. Buyer-fault returns do not reimburse completed original shipping. Shared shipping cannot be reimbursed twice across cases.
New transport budget / Justified seller compensationSeparate costs for this return. Compensation is normally zero; it needs an explanation, buyer agreement and admin review. It is not an automatic lost-profit penalty.
Buyer receivesThe actual net cash refund. With an agreed deduction, this is already reduced by the return budget. Do not pay the same fee again.
Accept agreementCheck the current version, complete financial breakdown and whether the item must come back. Tick confirmation only when you agree. A revised quote needs fresh acceptance.
Request admin reviewEnter at least 10 characters explaining the disagreement. This blocks new custody/refund steps until admin reviews it. Verified completed work can still be paid from its secured budget.
Private refund receiving accountBank/e-wallet, account holder and account number. Only buyer and admin can see the complete details. Never provide a PIN, password or OTP.
Verify refund accountCheck the account information and confirm it. Details are encrypted at rest and locked once admin unlocks funding. Changes before payment then require an admin account-correction review.
Funded refund accountThe verified account is locked once funding is unlocked. If it needs correction, request admin review. Admin can unlock a correction before payment; buyer re-verifies and admin reviews it again.
Pay return fee / Submit escrow paymentFor an agreed buyer-funded separate deposit, use the platform account/QR, pay exactly the outstanding amount, enter the real transfer reference and attach its receipt. This does not immediately unlock pickup.
I received the refundUse Return center after checking your actual bank/e-wallet account. A screenshot alone is not confirmation that you received money.
View replacement dispatch proof / Confirm replacement receivedReview the tracking reference and dispatch evidence. Confirm only after actually receiving the agreed replacement in the expected condition; otherwise request admin review.

In the buyer mobile app, open the case’s Agreement, fees & funding action. Your existing refund-proof and receipt-confirmation actions remain available.

Seller: decide, fund and schedule

Field / buttonHow to use it
ResponsibilityPropose Seller fault, Buyer selected incorrectly / change of mind, or Logistics damage or loss. Admin records the final review. Compare the saved purchased variation, not a listing edited afterward.
ResolutionChoose one of the four options above. For Exchange, enter zero cash refund. For Partial refund, enter the lower agreed amount. Return/refund and full refund-only use the eligible item value.
Who pays new transport?Seller, buyer (buyer-fault cases only), or platform (admin only). A seller can sponsor a buyer-fault return by choosing Seller.
Funding methodSeparate payment or, only when buyer pays, Deduct agreed fees from buyer refund. A deduction needs actual available escrow money; admin reserves it.
Item refund amount / Original shipping reimbursement / Justified seller compensationEnter the proposed amounts. Explain any compensation with evidence. Shipping reimbursement is limited to remaining paid shipping and is unavailable for buyer fault or exchanges.
Reason, evidence review and charge explanationWrite at least 10 characters explaining responsibility, amounts and the proposed outcome. Do not put private bank details here.
Replacement details — exchange onlySelect the agreed replacement variation and describe its expected size, color and condition. The same product is used; replacement stock is reserved before collection and restored once if the uncollected exchange is cancelled.
Send resolution proposalSaves a new version, resets approvals and any unfunded quote, and requests fresh agreement. A submitted or verified payment prevents silent rewriting.
Accept agreementSeller also accepts the complete final quote, after logistics has added the route fees. A disagreement on a new managed case goes to admin review; an older Reject submission cannot close a case holding verified funds.
Recover funds for buyer refundIf original proceeds were released or original completed logistics needs protection, fund the displayed refund shortfall separately. This is not the buyer paying for their own refund.
Schedule return pickupAfter funding unlocks, agree on availability with the buyer and enter the pickup date/time in Philippine time (PHT). Logistics assigns an approved collection rider.
Confirm physical receiptOnly after logistics dispatches the parcel back. Inspect the actual item. Tick “Inspected and resellable: restore this quantity to stock” only if appropriate. Stock restoration is allowed once.
Refund only / partial refundThere is no physical receipt action or collection schedule. The buyer keeps the item. Seller and buyer agree, funding is verified, and admin sends the net refund.

Do not press a transfer confirmation before actually sending money. New managed cases recover refund shortfalls through the verified agreement-payment ledger; do not count the same recovery again in the earlier inline recovery fields.

Riders, drivers, hubs and admin: accepting their cuts

The Return payments & earnings screen uses compact cards. Each shows service, return/item, amount and status. Open View recipient, payment and actions for secondary information; no wide horizontal table is needed.

Button / displayMeaning
Your confirmed return service earningsSum of your accepted service allocations only. A sent payment is not counted yet. Unused-budget refunds and seller compensation are not service earnings.
Verify remittance accountThe entitled rider/driver/hub representative enters bank/e-wallet, account holder and account number, then confirms accuracy. Details are encrypted and shown only to the recipient/admin.
Record return remittanceAdmin sends the eligible amount to the verified account, records a unique outgoing transfer reference and actual transfer proof. Status becomes Remitted.
View remittance proofPrivate image preview for admin and the entitled recipient.
Accept remittance receivedRecipient checks their account first, then confirms. Status becomes Accepted and the service amount enters confirmed return earnings. Repeated acceptance cannot credit twice.
View return agreementOpens the associated resolution, fees and funding. The right to view a remittance does not reveal the buyer’s private refund account.

Return service earnings have their own ledger. The original delivery’s existing earnings are not duplicated or deleted. Completed original work remains payable; commission on refunded merchandise is reversed. An exchange does not reverse merchandise revenue because it has no merchandise cash refund.

In the rider app, open Remittances and use the Return payments & earnings wallet action in the top bar. The inter-hub driver remittance screen has the same action. Logistics staff has the wallet action on the scanner screen. Return jobs now request a real handover photo before sending the custody update.

Three money examples

Amounts below are illustrative, not fixed transport prices.

Buyer-fault deduction

₱1,000 item refund

Agreed return transport ₱160 → buyer receives ₱840. ₱160 is reserved for actual services. Buyer does not pay another ₱160.

Seller-fault return

₱1,000 buyer refund

Seller separately funds ₱160 transport, plus any verified shortfall for the original refund. Applicable original shipping reimbursement is shown separately.

Seller waives return

₱1,000 buyer refund

No return fees. Buyer keeps the damaged item. No fictitious stock restoration, hub handoff or driver earnings.

Escrow accounting treats a deduction as movement into a separate return budget. Only cash actually sent to the buyer is recorded as the buyer’s refund transfer; the full agreed merchandise adjustment still reverses the correct seller proceeds and commission.

What prevents the common loopholes?

  • Fault is not inferred solely from the buyer’s selected reason. Both evidence and the original purchased variation matter; admin reviews disagreements.
  • Quotes are versioned. Changing an unfunded proposal clears approvals. Pending deposits and funded agreements cannot be silently rewritten.
  • Buyer charges are allowed only for agreed buyer-fault cases. Seller-sponsored free returns remain possible.
  • Bank details and transfer receipts are private. Nobody needs a password, PIN or OTP.
  • Funds are checked under an order lock. A screenshot is not verified cash; a ledger deduction cannot invent cash already paid out.
  • Each return service role has one allocation. Different hubs confirm actual transfers. Recipient acceptance is separate from admin transfer confirmation.
  • Completed original service earnings are protected. Return funds do not automatically become seller earnings.
  • Physical receipt and resellable-stock restoration are separate. Refund-only never creates fake stock.
  • The displayed 48-hour response target flags overdue cases for admin review; it does not automatically reject consumer claims, release money or fabricate receipt.
  • These new controls apply to physical-item return cases. Earlier prepaid cancellations with no physical quantity retain their separate existing refund-adjustment process.

Existing cases and manual checks

Cases already moving through the earlier return workflow continue that workflow. They are not silently assigned new transport charges. Earlier prepaid cancellations with no physical quantity also retain their existing refund adjustment. New physical-item cases and cases still awaiting collection use the agreement and funding controls.

Admin must check the actual bank or e-wallet account before verifying a deposit or recording a transfer. There is no automatic banking transfer in these buttons. If a payment is missing, use Request admin review; do not confirm receipt, upload the same transfer as a second payment, or invent a delivery milestone. Failed bank transfers and in-transit route changes require an admin investigation before another payment or route is recorded.
