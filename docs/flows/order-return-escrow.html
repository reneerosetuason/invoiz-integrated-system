Orders, returns & earnings

Invoiz process guide · November 5, 2026. Arrows show the normal sequence. Transfers are made manually and recorded with references and proof.

1. Ordering

  1. Buyer places orderChoose COD or GCash.
  2. GCash funding gateBuyer scans seller QR and uploads receipt → seller verifies and remits FULL total to escrow → admin verifies actual funds. COD continues without this GCash step.
  3. Seller accepts, prepares & packs
  4. Logistics routes and approves pickup
  5. Pickup rider collects from seller
  6. Origin hub receives, scans & sorts
  7. Transfer to another hub if neededManifest dispatch → destination hub confirms receipt.
  8. Delivery rider collects from hubAssigned rider, authorized dispatch and pickup.
  9. Buyer receives parcelRider records delivery photo. COD: record actual cash. GCash: collect NO cash.
  10. Payment reconciliation & settlement

2. Return / refund

  1. Buyer requests return with evidenceSpecific item, reason and eligible quantity.
  2. Seller reviewsApprove → tracked collection. Reject with explanation → buyer may ask support for review.
  3. Hub assigns collection riderRider confirms physical collection. No cash.
  4. Hub confirms return receiptRecord any return transfer and receiving-hub confirmation.
  5. Logistics dispatches back to sellerRecord transport / handover reference.
  6. Seller receives & inspectsRestore stock only if marked resellable.
  7. Admin checks refund fundingRecover any shortfall into escrow first. Preserve fees for completed logistics work.
  8. Admin transfers refund to buyerExact amount, real reference, private proof and verification. This records REFUND SENT.
  9. Buyer confirms receiptCheck the receiving account first. Then the case closes.
  10. Remaining eligible payouts resumeCancelled prepaid orders enter refund review directly, including unused shipping. No second stock restoration.

3. Money & earnings

  1. Payment method — choose one path
    GCash
    Buyer → seller → FULL escrow deposit. Admin verifies incoming funds.    COD
    Buyer → delivery rider. Retain eligible last-mile cut and reconcile remaining cash.
    Both paths feed the allocations below. A buyer's GCash receipt alone does not unlock acceptance.
  2. Allocate by verified work and route
    Admin
    Net merchandise commission    Seller
    Remaining product proceeds    Origin hub
    Configured hub fee    Pickup rider
    Configured per-item fee    Inter-hub driver
    Eligible transport fee    Destination hub
    Eligible receiving fee    Delivery rider
    Last-mile fee
    GCash allocations stay held until delivery. COD pickup earnings can follow verified hub handoff.
  3. Open return / dispute?Hold further payouts. Complete the refund and any required recovery first. Reverse the refunded merchandise commission.
  4. Release only eligible, funded amountsGCash: individual actual transfer reference per allocation. Refunds reduce the available escrow balance.
  5. Recipient confirms where applicableA rider's GCash earning is credited only after that rider accepts the incoming remittance.
  6. Record onceNo GCash cash deduction, duplicate refund, duplicate stock restoration or second forward-delivery earning for a reverse collection.
