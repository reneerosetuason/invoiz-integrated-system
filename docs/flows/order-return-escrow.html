Orders, returns & earnings

Invoiz process guide · November 9, 2026. Arrows show the normal sequence. Transfers are made manually and recorded with references and proof. Open the detailed return/refund button guide.

1. Ordering

  1. Buyer places orderChoose COD or GCash.
  2. GCash funding gateBuyer scans seller QR and uploads receipt → seller verifies and remits FULL total to escrow → admin verifies actual funds. COD continues without this GCash step.
  3. Seller accepts, prepares & packs
  4. Logistics routes and approves pickup
  5. Pickup rider collects from seller
  6. Origin hub receives, scans & sorts
  7. Transfer to another hub if neededManifest dispatch → destination hub confirms receipt.
  8. Delivery rider collects from hubAssigned rider, authorized dispatch and pickup.
  9. Buyer receives parcelRider records delivery photo. COD: record actual cash. GCash: collect NO cash.
  10. Payment reconciliation & settlement

2. Return / refund

  1. Buyer requests return with evidenceSpecific item, reason and eligible quantity.
  2. Agree resolution and responsibilitySeller proposes the remedy; the system calculates all required service fees from Commission settings. Buyer accepts, declines, or suggests changes. Seller revises until both agree; no admin responsibility decision is required.
  3. New buyer-fault return: deductions and seller depositSystem deducts original seller-funded costs plus new return delivery. Seller deposits only the new delivery fee and buyer saves a receiving account. Receipt submission authorizes collection; actual bank verification is still required before service payouts. Seller-fault and existing paid cases retain their recorded funding process.
  4. Buyer keeps item?Refund-only / partial refund skip physical custody and stock restoration. Admin sends the agreed net refund; buyer confirms receipt.
  5. Hub assigns online collection riderRider opens Return jobs on Home or Deliveries and confirms physical collection with a photo. No cash.
  6. Return transfer if neededOriginal seller hub is recommended; another active receiving hub may be selected while preserving agreed fees. Collection-company driver opens Return transfers, accepts and records arrival. Receiving hub separately confirms intake.
  7. Receiving hub dispatches online rider back to sellerThe assigned rider opens Return jobs and records the actual seller handover. Seller independently confirms receipt.
  8. Seller receives & inspectsRestore stock only if marked resellable.
  9. Exchange?Reserve replacement stock before collection. Record replacement dispatch, variation and rider. Buyer confirms receipt; no second sale or COD collection.
  10. Seller sends the new buyer-fault refund directlyAfter physical receipt, seller sends the agreed net refund and records proof. Buyer checks actual money received and confirms. Example: ₱4,599 − ₱569.90 original costs − ₱160 return = ₱3,869.10. Only ₱160 goes to delivery escrow. Other remedies keep their existing refund funding checks.
  11. Record the actual refund sentSeller records the new buyer-fault direct refund. Payment staff handles unchanged seller-fault and earlier platform-funded remedies. Use the exact amount, real reference and private proof.
  12. Buyer confirms receiptCheck the receiving account first. Then the case closes.
  13. Remaining eligible payouts resumeCancelled prepaid orders enter refund review directly, including unused shipping. No second stock restoration.

3. Money & earnings

  1. Payment method — choose one path
    GCash
    Buyer → seller → FULL escrow deposit. Admin verifies incoming funds.    COD
    Buyer → delivery rider. Retain eligible last-mile cut and reconcile remaining cash.
    Both paths feed the allocations below. A buyer's GCash receipt alone does not unlock acceptance.
  2. Allocate by verified work and route
    Admin
    Net merchandise commission    Seller
    Remaining product proceeds    Origin hub
    Configured hub fee    Pickup rider
    Configured per-item fee    Inter-hub driver
    Eligible transport fee    Destination hub
    Eligible receiving fee    Delivery rider
    Last-mile fee
    GCash allocations stay held until delivery. COD pickup earnings can follow verified hub handoff.
  3. Open return / dispute?Hold further original payouts. New buyer-fault cost recovery preserves the original earned commission and delivery fees. Other merchandise refunds follow their existing commission reversal and recovery rules.
  4. Order and return shares in the same remittancesCommission-priced return shares become ready after work and funding are confirmed. Admin releases them in Manage Payments. Each recipient confirms in the regular remittance list; accepted return pay updates normal earnings. Preserve completed original-delivery earnings.
  5. Release only eligible, funded amountsUse an individual actual transfer reference per allocation. Only platform-paid refunds reduce platform escrow. A seller's direct external refund is recorded separately.
  6. Recipient confirms where applicableA rider's GCash earning is credited only after that rider accepts the incoming remittance.
  7. Record onceNo GCash cash deduction, duplicate refund, duplicate stock restoration or second forward-delivery earning for a reverse collection.
