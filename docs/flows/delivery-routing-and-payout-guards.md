# Destination routing and remittance release

Last-mile assignment uses the buyer destination and the hubs' registered service areas. Missing destination IDs no longer mean that an order is automatically local to the origin hub. Active service areas supply a destination suggestion; otherwise a unique active hub in the buyer's city/province can supply it. Ambiguous locations need staff review. No buyer address is sent to an external routing service by these checks.

Seller pickup remains available at the origin. After hub receipt, a parcel outside the handling hub's local coverage cannot be assigned, accepted, collected or advanced for last-mile delivery. It must travel through the inter-hub workflow and be received, scanned and sorted at its final hub. The same checks run on individual and bulk assignment, web actions and rider API actions. Sorting cannot label an uncovered remote buyer as a local destination.

For an existing uncollected assignment with an incorrect destination, handling staff can open the parcel and choose **Review destination hub**. This records an event, clears the uncollected delivery assignment and uses the suggested destination. An already reviewed destination at a different hub is preserved. Active transfers and parcels already collected from the hub cannot be changed through this action. Physical receipt, trip completion, payment and delivery are never fabricated.

External remittance recipients need complete saved provider/bank, account-holder and account-number details. Hub earnings use the hub profile, truck drivers use their driver profile, and sellers/riders use their user profile. Existing complete per-return receiving details remain valid. Bank details are checked again on the server before individual or return-service release; a direct POST cannot bypass the requirement. COD batches skip incomplete recipients, leave their records pending, and display only eligible recipients in the batch total. Missing-account rows show an explanation instead of a release form. After a recipient saves their account, refresh the Payments page.

Platform commission retained internally is not an external bank transfer and retains its existing collection/acceptance flow. Funding verification, completed-work requirements, dispute holds, references, transfer evidence and recipient acceptance remain separate checks. Existing remitted/accepted payments are not reset.
